# M3-06.4 Family Adventure Intelligence Plan

## Status

**Milestone:** M3-06.4 Family Adventure Intelligence
**Project:** Adventure Companion
**Status:** Design in progress
**Depends on:**

- M3-06.1 Adventure Brain Foundation
- M3-06.2 Dynamic Dashboard
- M3-06.3 Living Campfire

---

## 1. Purpose

Family Adventure Intelligence extends Adventure Companion’s existing intelligence architecture from trip-level awareness to person-aware and family-aware understanding.

Adventure Brain already determines what deserves attention across the adventure.

The Dynamic Dashboard presents one calm operational focus.

Living Campfire interprets Adventure Brain’s communication contract and responds in Remy’s supportive voice.

Family Adventure Intelligence adds the ability to understand:

- which adventurers are represented in the available data
- the readiness state of each adventurer
- meaningful individual readiness accomplishments
- whether the entire family is ready
- which individual or family milestone should receive attention first

Family Adventure Intelligence answers:

> Who needs preparation, encouragement, recognition, or celebration right now?

It does not create a separate Family Brain.

It does not independently calculate readiness inside presentation components.

It does not compare, rank, shame, or criticize family members.

Instead, it extends the single Adventure Brain with deterministic family reasoning that can be safely consumed by the Dynamic Dashboard, Living Campfire, and future experience surfaces.

---

## 2. Experience Philosophy

Family Adventure Intelligence should help every adventurer feel seen without turning readiness into competition.

The experience follows seven principles.

### 2.1 Individual before family

When an individual readiness accomplishment and a whole-family readiness accomplishment are both eligible, the individual accomplishment must be surfaced first.

This preserves the meaning of the person’s achievement instead of allowing it to be immediately overshadowed by the larger family milestone.

The required order is:

1. newly ready individual
2. other meaningful individual readiness insight
3. whole-family readiness

This ordering must be deterministic.

### 2.2 Supportive, never comparative

Family Adventure Intelligence must not rank people against one another.

It must not describe any adventurer as:

- behind
- least prepared
- holding up the family
- failing
- slower than another person
- responsible for family unreadiness

The experience may describe a person’s own readiness state, but it must not turn that state into a comparison.

### 2.3 Grounded in structured data

Family Adventure Intelligence must not invent:

- readiness changes
- completed packing items
- missing items
- emotional states
- family relationships
- accomplishments
- participation
- preferences
- responsibilities

Every family or individual conclusion must be supported by normalized structured state.

### 2.4 One calm family insight

The system may evaluate multiple adventurers and multiple eligible family insights.

It must expose only one primary family insight at a time.

The user should not receive several simultaneous readiness messages, celebrations, warnings, or encouragement prompts.

### 2.5 Stable identity

Family reasoning must use stable adventurer identifiers.

It must not depend only on:

- display names
- array position
- presentation order

Display names may change without changing the identity of the adventurer.

### 2.6 Inclusive family recognition

All configured adventurers must be eligible for appropriate recognition.

Family-level reasoning must not silently exclude Bubbe, Papa, or future adventurers simply because they were added after the first readiness features were created.

### 2.7 Deterministic behavior

Given the same normalized input, Family Adventure Intelligence must always produce the same output.

It must not use:

- random selection
- unstable object iteration
- hidden presentation-state ordering
- implicit current-time behavior
- nondeterministic tie-breaking

Stable tie-breaking rules must be defined and tested.

## 3. Architectural Responsibilities

Family Adventure Intelligence extends the existing Adventure Companion architecture without creating a parallel intelligence path.

### 3.1 Structured source data

Existing application data remains responsible for factual information such as:

- configured adventurers
- stable adventurer identifiers
- packing assignments
- completed packing items
- readiness-relevant requirements
- itinerary participation
- reservation participation
- other explicit person-level state

Source data must not contain presentation wording or decide which insight should be shown.

### 3.2 Shared state

`shared-state.js` is responsible for normalizing family-related source data into a predictable contract.

Shared state should provide:

- a stable collection of adventurers
- stable adventurer identifiers
- display names
- normalized individual readiness information
- explicit availability states
- deterministic ordering inputs

Shared state should not:

- write Remy messages
- select celebrations
- rank eligible insights
- decide whether an individual or family milestone appears first

### 3.3 Adventure Brain

`adventure-brain.js` is the single owner of family reasoning.

Adventure Brain is responsible for:

- evaluating individual readiness
- detecting eligible individual accomplishments
- determining whole-family readiness
- ranking individual and family insights
- applying stable tie-breaking
- enforcing individual-before-family ordering
- exposing one normalized family intelligence result

Adventure Brain must not depend on presentation markup or DOM state.

### 3.4 Living Campfire

`living-campfire.js` is responsible for translating an eligible family communication contract into Remy’s voice.

Living Campfire may:

- recognize an individual accomplishment
- celebrate a whole-family milestone
- offer calm encouragement
- describe a supported next step

Living Campfire must not independently recalculate readiness or choose which adventurer deserves attention.

### 3.5 Dynamic Dashboard

The Dynamic Dashboard may display the family insight selected by Adventure Brain when that insight is appropriate for the current Daily Focus.

The Dashboard must not:

- scan raw packing data
- calculate family readiness
- choose between adventurers
- override the Brain’s ranking

### 3.6 Presentation and diagnostics

Presentation code is responsible only for rendering the selected result safely and accessibly.

Diagnostics may expose family intelligence inputs and outputs for development verification, but diagnostics must not become a second source of family logic.

## 4. Family Intelligence Contract

Adventure Brain should expose one normalized family intelligence object.

Presentation components consume this object rather than independently evaluating family readiness.

The exact implementation may evolve during development, but the contract should remain conceptually similar to:

```text
familyIntelligence
├── state
├── insight
├── priority
├── individuals
├── familyReadiness
├── selectedAdventurer
└── metadata
```

### 4.1 State

The overall availability of family intelligence.

Possible states include:

- unavailable
- available
- unknown

### 4.2 Insight

The single highest-priority family insight selected by Adventure Brain.

Examples include:

- newly ready individual
- remaining readiness step
- family ready
- encouragement
- no meaningful family insight

Only one primary insight should be exposed at a time.

### 4.3 Individuals

A normalized collection describing each adventurer's evaluated readiness state.

This collection is intended for deterministic reasoning rather than presentation formatting.

### 4.4 Family readiness

A normalized representation of the overall family readiness state.

This should answer questions such as:

- Is everyone ready?
- Is readiness information incomplete?
- Is a family celebration appropriate?

### 4.5 Selected adventurer

When an individual insight is chosen, Adventure Brain should identify the associated adventurer using a stable identifier.

Presentation components may use the display name for rendering, but the intelligence contract should remain identity-based.

### 4.6 Metadata

Supporting information that assists diagnostics, deterministic testing, and future enhancements without affecting presentation behavior.

## 5. Eligibility and Priority Rules

Family Adventure Intelligence may evaluate several possible individual and family insights from the same normalized state.

Adventure Brain must select one result using explicit eligibility, priority, and tie-breaking rules.

### 5.1 Eligibility

An insight is eligible only when all information required to support it is available.

An individual insight requires:

- a valid stable adventurer identifier
- a display name
- sufficient readiness information
- a supported readiness conclusion

A whole-family insight requires:

- at least one configured adventurer
- sufficient readiness information for every included adventurer
- a supported whole-family conclusion

Unknown or incomplete information must not be treated as either ready or not ready.

### 5.2 Family insight priority

Eligible family insights should be evaluated in this order:

1. newly ready individual
2. other meaningful individual readiness accomplishment
3. important individual preparation need
4. whole-family readiness
5. calm family encouragement
6. no meaningful family insight

This order applies within Family Adventure Intelligence.

The existing Adventure Brain priority tiers continue to decide whether a family insight outranks unrelated safety, weather, reservation, departure, or other trip-level intelligence.

### 5.3 Individual-before-family rule

When an individual accomplishment and whole-family readiness become eligible from the same evaluated state, the individual accomplishment must be selected first.

Whole-family readiness may become eligible for a later experience after the individual accomplishment has been recognized through supported state or history.

The implementation must not rely on hidden DOM state to enforce this order.

### 5.4 Stable tie-breaking

When multiple adventurers have equally eligible insights, Adventure Brain must use stable tie-breaking.

Tie-breaking should use:

1. explicit configured adventurer order, when available
2. stable adventurer identifier
3. no additional nondeterministic fallback

Display name must not be the primary identity or tie-breaking key.

### 5.5 No unsupported change detection

The system must not describe an adventurer as newly ready unless the available state can support a transition or an explicit unrecognized milestone.

A current ready state alone does not prove that readiness changed during the current session.

If transition history is unavailable, the system should use supported present-state language rather than inventing a new accomplishment.

### 5.6 Calm fallback behavior

When no meaningful family insight is eligible, Adventure Brain should return an explicit neutral result rather than:

- selecting arbitrary encouragement
- inventing a readiness change
- repeating a stale celebration
- throwing an error
- returning an inconsistent partial object

## 6. State Model and Required Inputs

Family Adventure Intelligence should operate only on normalized, explicit state.

It must not infer person-level readiness from unrelated presentation text or DOM structure.

### 6.1 Adventurer identity

Each adventurer should provide:

- a stable identifier
- a display name
- a deterministic configured order when available
- an explicit participation state when relevant

The stable identifier is the authoritative identity.

### 6.2 Individual readiness

Each evaluated adventurer should have a normalized readiness record that can distinguish among:

- available readiness data
- unavailable readiness data
- unknown readiness state
- ready
- not ready
- partially ready, when supported by the source model

Readiness should not be inferred from a missing object or empty array without an explicit normalization rule.

### 6.3 Readiness evidence

When available, the normalized model may include evidence such as:

- total required items
- completed required items
- incomplete required items
- blocking requirements
- readiness percentage
- explicit completion state

Evidence must remain factual and should not include presentation wording.

### 6.4 Transition and recognition state

A newly ready or newly completed milestone requires support beyond a current-state snapshot.

The architecture may support this through:

- previous normalized state
- explicit milestone history
- a persisted recognition record
- another deterministic transition contract

Until transition support is confirmed, the Brain must not claim that a current ready state is newly achieved.

### 6.5 Whole-family inclusion

Whole-family readiness must define which configured adventurers are included.

The inclusion rule should be explicit and deterministic.

An adventurer must not be excluded merely because:

- the person has no packing assignments
- the person was added later
- the person appears after another adventurer in the source data
- a presentation component does not currently render that person

Unavailable or unknown readiness data must be handled explicitly rather than silently ignored.

### 6.6 Empty and partial states

The normalized state must safely support:

- no configured adventurers
- one configured adventurer
- multiple adventurers
- missing readiness data
- mixed available and unavailable readiness data
- duplicate or invalid identifiers
- unsupported source values

Invalid or incomplete source data should degrade to an explicit safe state rather than causing presentation failure.

## 7. Testing Strategy and Acceptance Criteria

Family Adventure Intelligence must be supported by focused unit tests and full regression coverage.

Tests should prove both correct selection and safe fallback behavior.

### 7.1 Shared-state tests

`shared-state.test.js` should verify that normalized family state:

- preserves stable adventurer identifiers
- preserves deterministic configured order
- distinguishes available, unavailable, and unknown readiness data
- handles no adventurers safely
- handles one adventurer safely
- handles multiple adventurers safely
- handles missing readiness records safely
- handles invalid or duplicate identifiers deterministically
- does not invent readiness conclusions from absent data

### 7.2 Adventure Brain unit tests

Adventure Brain tests should verify:

- individual readiness is evaluated from normalized state
- only supported insights become eligible
- an individual accomplishment outranks simultaneous whole-family readiness
- important individual preparation needs are ranked consistently
- whole-family readiness requires sufficient information for every included adventurer
- unknown readiness is not treated as ready
- unavailable readiness is not silently ignored
- equal-priority individual insights use stable tie-breaking
- the same input always produces the same output
- the neutral fallback result is complete and predictable

### 7.3 Transition and recognition tests

When transition or recognition support is introduced, tests should verify:

- current readiness alone is not labeled newly ready
- a supported readiness transition can produce a newly ready insight
- an already recognized individual milestone is not repeatedly selected
- whole-family recognition does not precede an eligible individual recognition
- recognition behavior does not depend on DOM state
- persisted or previous state is interpreted deterministically

### 7.4 Living Campfire tests

`living-campfire.test.js` should verify that family communication:

- uses only the Adventure Brain communication contract
- does not recalculate readiness
- includes the selected adventurer only when supported
- produces supportive, non-comparative wording
- does not shame or blame an adventurer
- distinguishes individual recognition from whole-family celebration
- safely handles unavailable or neutral family intelligence
- remains deterministic for identical inputs

### 7.5 Integration tests

Integration coverage should verify:

- shared state feeds family intelligence correctly
- Adventure Brain exposes one normalized family result
- the Dynamic Dashboard does not independently calculate family readiness
- Living Campfire consumes the selected family result
- Bubbe and Papa remain eligible for individual and family recognition
- existing weather, reservation, departure, packing, and Daily Focus behavior does not regress

### 7.6 Acceptance criteria

M3-06.4 is complete when all of the following are true:

- Adventure Brain remains the single owner of family reasoning.
- Every included adventurer is represented by a stable identifier.
- Family readiness uses explicit normalized state.
- Unknown and unavailable readiness states are handled safely.
- One primary family insight is selected at a time.
- Individual recognition is selected before simultaneous family recognition.
- Equal-priority results use deterministic tie-breaking.
- No family member is ranked, blamed, shamed, or described comparatively.
- Living Campfire does not duplicate readiness calculations.
- Dashboard presentation does not duplicate readiness calculations.
- Bubbe and Papa are included in supported family-level behavior.
- Unit, integration, and regression tests pass.
- Identical input produces identical output.
- The application loads normally with no new console errors.

## 8. Deferred Features and Milestone Boundaries

M3-06.4 establishes deterministic individual and family readiness intelligence.

The following capabilities are intentionally deferred unless implementation reveals that a minimal supporting piece is required for the approved acceptance criteria.

### 8.1 Rich behavioral profiles

This milestone will not create personality profiles, emotional profiles, motivation scores, or behavioral predictions for individual adventurers.

Family Adventure Intelligence should reason only from explicit structured adventure data.

### 8.2 Comparative readiness displays

This milestone will not introduce:

* readiness leaderboards
* best-prepared rankings
* least-prepared rankings
* competitive scores
* public comparisons among adventurers

Individual readiness may be represented, but it must remain supportive and non-comparative.

### 8.3 Responsibility assignment

This milestone will not automatically assign chores, packing tasks, reminders, or responsibilities to family members.

Future assignment features must use explicit user-controlled data and must not infer responsibility from age, family role, or prior behavior.

### 8.4 Emotion inference

This milestone will not infer that an adventurer is:

* anxious
* excited
* reluctant
* overwhelmed
* disappointed
* frustrated
* disengaged

Any future emotion-aware behavior must be grounded in explicit user-provided state and separately designed privacy safeguards.

### 8.5 Personalized recommendation engines

This milestone will not independently recommend activities, food, pacing, or itinerary changes for specific family members based on inferred preferences.

Existing explicit itinerary, accessibility, participation, and preference data may continue to be used by their current owning systems.

### 8.6 Cross-device family accounts

This milestone will not create:

* separate user accounts for each adventurer
* authentication
* shared cloud profiles
* family invitations
* cross-device recognition history
* multi-user conflict resolution

Family Intelligence will operate within the application’s existing local data model.

### 8.7 Notification systems

This milestone will not add push notifications, email notifications, text messages, or background reminders.

The selected family insight will appear only through existing Adventure Companion experience surfaces.

### 8.8 Unlimited milestone history

This milestone will not build a general-purpose event ledger or permanent family achievement archive.

If recognition history is required to enforce individual-before-family behavior, it should use the smallest deterministic state contract needed for this milestone.

### 8.9 Generative family messaging

Living Campfire family messages will continue to come from deterministic templates and supported context.

This milestone will not introduce uncontrolled generated text or messaging that can invent family facts.

### 8.10 New intelligence engine

M3-06.4 will not create a separate Family Brain, readiness engine, or presentation-owned intelligence system.

All family reasoning remains an extension of the single Adventure Brain.

## 9. Proposed Implementation Sequence

Implementation should proceed through small, independently testable commits.

### Commit 1 — Architecture plan

Add and review this M3-06.4 architecture document.

### Commit 2 — Current-state inventory and normalized family inputs

Inspect the existing adventurer, packing, readiness, and shared-state contracts.

Add only the normalization changes required to expose stable family inputs.

### Commit 3 — Pure family readiness helpers

Add deterministic helpers for:

* individual readiness evaluation
* whole-family readiness evaluation
* eligibility checks
* stable adventurer ordering

### Commit 4 — Family insight candidates

Create normalized candidate insights without changing presentation behavior.

### Commit 5 — Priority and tie-breaking

Select one family insight using explicit priority and stable tie-breaking rules.

### Commit 6 — Recognition sequencing

Add the smallest supported transition or recognition contract required to enforce individual-before-family behavior.

### Commit 7 — Adventure Brain contract integration

Expose the selected family intelligence result through the existing Adventure Brain output.

### Commit 8 — Living Campfire integration

Translate eligible family intelligence into deterministic, supportive Remy messaging.

### Commit 9 — Dynamic Dashboard integration

Allow the existing Dashboard pipeline to present family intelligence without duplicating family logic.

### Commit 10 — Regression, diagnostics, and documentation

Complete full-suite tests, visual verification, diagnostic review, and any required documentation updates.

M3-06.4 is done when:

* the approved family intelligence contract is implemented
* normalized family inputs are explicit and deterministic
* Adventure Brain evaluates individual and whole-family readiness
* one primary family insight is selected
* individual recognition precedes simultaneous family recognition
* stable identity and tie-breaking are enforced
* Living Campfire consumes rather than recalculates family intelligence
* Dashboard presentation consumes rather than recalculates family intelligence
* all configured adventurers remain eligible for supported recognition
* the experience is supportive and non-comparative
* unit, integration, and regression tests pass
* the application loads normally
* no new console errors are introduced
* implementation matches this architecture or documented amendments explain any intentional change
